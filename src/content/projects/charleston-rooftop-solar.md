Before Axis Energy became a dedicated renewable EPC and O&M company, its team built its capabilities inside White Electrical's Renewable Energy Division. On Boeing's Building 88-30, the 787 Final Assembly Building in North Charleston, that team worked with Baker Renewable Energy, which led the delivery, to install a 2.6 MWdc rooftop array: more than 18,000 lightweight thin-film laminates across about 10 acres of membrane roof.

The low-profile, non-reflective laminates suited a broad roof near airport operations, and the work called for safe rooftop execution, repeatable installation quality and organized electrical pathways around a major manufacturing asset. The array was completed in December 2011 and supported Boeing's renewable-energy strategy for its South Carolina site.

*A heritage project: delivered by the White Electrical Construction Company renewable division, which became Axis Energy in 2017.*
