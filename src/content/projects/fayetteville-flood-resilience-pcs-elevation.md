An extreme storm in September 2018 left ten central inverter stations at Innovative Solar 42, near Fayetteville, North Carolina, standing in floodwater. Rather than replace the equipment, the owner raised it: each power conversion station was lifted onto a vault-supported design about 32 inches above its old grade.

Axis Energy carried the electrical work around that retrofit in 2019. Crews safed off and de-terminated each station, preserved the 35 kV collection circuit through construction, extended four sizes of medium-voltage aluminum cable with 60 submersible splice kits and 60 loadbreak elbows, rebuilt the fiber network with fusion splices and new terminations, and tested every interface before staged re-energization.

Innovative Solar 42 shows how Axis supports flood-damaged generation assets: a coordinated elevation and electrical-recovery plan that preserves valuable equipment, controls outage risk and establishes tested connections for return to service.
