Solar 55, a 2.0 MWac five-inverter facility in Mount Olive, North Carolina, was underperforming after years of deficient installation practices and incomplete maintenance. A 2018 aerial infrared scan found one inverter offline, eight strings producing nothing, eight modules with active bypass-diode faults and a module knocked out of its racking: 14.7 percent of the array's capacity affected.

Axis Energy combined that thermal data with hands-on inspection to separate isolated component faults from systemic deficiencies, then turned the findings into a prioritized work plan. Over several months the crews corrected the racking and module damage, rerouted and supported conductors, troubleshot connectors, strings and the bi-polar inverters, brought the vegetation under control and retested the site.

Solar 55 shows how Axis takes control of a distressed asset, establishes its true condition, resolves the defects suppressing production and returns the facility with a documented basis for long-term O&M.
