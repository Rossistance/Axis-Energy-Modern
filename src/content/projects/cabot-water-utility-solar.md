Axis Energy built a 4.8 MWdc solar facility in Cabot, Arkansas, for a public water utility. More than 11,000 modules sit on a ground-mounted, single-axis tracking system across a 30-acre site, and the facility began generating power in May 2022.

The project established a new regulatory pathway for public-utility renewable energy in Arkansas: it was the first facility above 1 MW approved for net metering by the Arkansas Public Service Commission. It is expected to offset approximately 20 percent of the utility's energy expense.
