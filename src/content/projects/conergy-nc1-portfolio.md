In September of 2015 Axis Energy partnered with Conergy Projects Group to construct a 7-site 38 MWDC portfolio spread out over a 250 mile footprint in North Carolina. Despite logistical challenges associated with the distance between sites, Axis was able to successfully commission all 7 projects on time and within budget.

Combined, the NC-1 portfolio contributed over $35 million of sustainable energy investment in local communities across the state of North Carolina.
