Axis Energy and White Electrical Construction combined renewable project delivery with self-performed electrical construction to build a 423.20 kWdc solar canopy system across Boehringer Ingelheim Animal Health's operating campus in Gainesville, Georgia.

Six canopy structures had to work as one generating plant while keeping clearances, traffic flow, lighting and equipment access in order on a pharmaceutical and animal-health campus. Beneath the roadways and inside two occupied buildings, the team added disconnects, distribution equipment, breakers, conduit and wiring, rerouted circuits and completed the testing for the behind-the-meter connections at the P1B and R&D buildings.
