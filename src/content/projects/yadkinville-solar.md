A storm left this 4.808 MWdc fixed-tilt solar facility in Yadkinville, North Carolina, with failed posts, damaged racking and compromised wiring. Axis Energy took control of the site, established safe shutdown and electrical isolation, and carried the plant through a complete demolition and rebuild.

The recovery moved row by row: module, rack and post removal; resurvey and new foundations; RBI GM-2 replacement racking; module reinstallation and DC restoration; then infrared testing, torque verification and QA/QC. Along the way the crews resolved buried conduit conflicts, posts that could not be pulled, and the access limits of working inside an existing plant.

Yadkinville shows the repowering, storm restoration and brownfield construction capability Axis brings to asset owners: one accountable team from forensic field assessment and safe demolition through structural rebuild, electrical restoration, testing and turnover.
