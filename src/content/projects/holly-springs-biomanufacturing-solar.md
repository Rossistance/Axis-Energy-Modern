Axis Energy delivered rooftop and ground-mount solar for a greenfield pharmaceutical biomanufacturing campus in Holly Springs, North Carolina. The assignment joined two distinct array environments into one coordinated delivery program, combining process-campus safety, roof access and equipment congestion, civil fieldwork, distributed string inverters, medium-voltage collection, testing and turnover.

The campus pairs traditional stainless-steel fed-batch manufacturing with next-generation single-use technology, and the solar scope called for the same integration mindset. Axis coordinated the rooftop and ground-mount arrays, worked around evolving facility interfaces, kept disciplined quality records and connected the PV systems through the campus's 12.47 kV electrical system. It was Axis's first pharmaceutical project.
