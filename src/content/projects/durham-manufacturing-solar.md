White Electrical's renewable division, the team that became Axis Energy in 2017, turned a solar package first designed and procured for a GE facility in South America into a site-specific, behind-the-meter system for GE Aviation's engine-manufacturing campus in Durham, North Carolina (GE Aerospace since 2024).

Work began in August 2014. Pervasive bedrock ruled out driven piles, so the array stands on drilled piers with rebar-reinforced concrete footers. The 1.2 MWdc fixed-tilt array pairs CIGS thin-film modules with GE central inverters, and the work was phased around an aircraft-engine plant that runs around the clock and the sheep that manage the site's vegetation. The system was commissioned in December 2015.

*Delivered by the White Electrical Construction Company renewable division, which became Axis Energy in 2017.*
