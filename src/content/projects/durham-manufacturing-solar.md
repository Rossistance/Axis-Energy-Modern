In August of 2014 Axis Energy began work on a 1.2 MWDC ground-mount, fixed-tilt PV system at a manufacturing plant in Durham, NC. Axis provided construction management services throughout the project life cycle, delivering a successful installation capable of generating enough energy each year to power 100 average-sized homes.

*Delivered by the White Electrical Construction Company renewable division, which became Axis Energy in 2017.*
