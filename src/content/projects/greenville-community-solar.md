Axis Energy delivered a 654 kWdc / 499 kWac community solar facility for Greenville Utilities Commission in Greenville, North Carolina, as EPC contractor, from engineering through construction and commissioning in 2025. The fixed-tilt array uses 1,118 bifacial modules and five string inverters, and Axis now maintains it under an O&M agreement.
