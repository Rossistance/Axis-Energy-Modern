In April 2019 a thermal event destroyed one of the 3 MW Power Electronics HEMK central inverters at Edenton Solar in North Carolina. The fire did not stop at the cabinet: it damaged the DC equipment, the feeder conductors running to the combiner clusters, and parts of the conduit system under the skid.

Axis Energy defined the true damage boundary, then ran a disciplined replacement program over two and a half weeks of planned field work: controlled demolition, removal of the damaged feeders, excavation and conduit rehabilitation, new power, grounding and Cat6 communications cabling, integration of the replacement inverter, and megger testing before turnover.

Edenton shows how Axis mobilizes after a major equipment event and returns the affected inverter block with tested electrical infrastructure and clear closeout documentation.
