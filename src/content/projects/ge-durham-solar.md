In August of 2014 Axis Energy collaborated with GE Power & Water to construct a 1.2 MWDC Ground Mount fixed tilt PV system at the GE Aviation Manufacturing plant in Durham, NC. Axis provided Construction Management services throughout the project life cycle, delivering a successful installation capable of generating enough energy each year to power 100 average-sized homes.

*Delivered by the White Electrical Construction Company renewable division, which became Axis Energy in 2017.*
