At GE HealthCare's MRI magnet manufacturing campus in Florence, South Carolina, a former employee parking lot sat underused. Axis Energy turned it into a 1.76 MWdc solar facility connected behind the meter, so the power it generates offsets electricity the plant would otherwise buy, without clearing any undeveloped land.

Building on existing pavement meant fitting the design to what was already there: grades, drainage, access, buried utilities and an operating manufacturing campus. Axis coordinated the civil, structural and electrical work, aligned the racking, inverters, collection and facility point of connection, and carried the project through testing, energization, documentation and owner acceptance.

The project shows how an industrial owner can reclaim an idle paved asset and put generation right where the energy is used, with one accountable EPC team.
