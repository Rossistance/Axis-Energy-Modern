In May of 2011 South Carolina Electric & Gas, Baker Renewable Energy and Axis Energy partnered to construct a 2.6 MWDC rooftop photovoltaic system on the newly built Boeing Dreamliner manufacturing facility in Charleston, SC. In total, 18,000 thin-film laminate modules were used to cover approximately 10 acres of roof space atop the facility. The installation will provide the Boeing Dreamliner facility years of clean renewable energy.

*Delivered by the White Electrical Construction Company renewable division, which became Axis Energy in 2017.*
