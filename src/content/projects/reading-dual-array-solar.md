Axis Energy delivered two distinct ground-mount solar systems for EnerSys' campus in Reading, Pennsylvania, each engineered around a different building, array technology and medium-voltage point of interconnection. A 3.004 MWdc single-axis tracker array serves the headquarters, and a separate 2.21 MWdc fixed-tilt array serves the adjacent Global Technology Center.

The work ran from rock removal and foundations through more than 1,200 feet of routing to each building's switchgear, protection, controls integration with the building automation system, testing and energization, on a campus that stayed in operation throughout. The installation counts 9,568 panels across about 6.1 acres, and EnerSys estimates it generates about 7.3 GWh of renewable electricity a year.
