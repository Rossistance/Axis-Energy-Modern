# Design concept archive

These four concept documents (Atlas, Aurora, Canopy, Horizon) and their single-page previews were produced during an earlier exploration in October 2025. None shipped as-is. The 2026 redesign takes the editorial light layout from **Atlas** and the illuminated gradient bands and glass cards from **Aurora**, built on the official brand colours (PANTONE 288 blue, PANTONE 348 green) and the 2025 hero-art set.

The previews reference the old stylesheet and contain illustrative statistics that are not company facts; treat them as sketches only. The live design system is in `src/styles/tokens.css`.
